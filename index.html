Questions engineers ask

How is this different from PagerDuty or Datadog?

They route the page and show the dashboards. Vigilante is the engineer who picks it up — reading those same signals, testing hypotheses, and returning a cause and a fix. It connects to your existing tools instead of replacing them.

Does it touch production?

Not until you let it. Vigilante is read-only by default. You move it up the autonomy ladder as it earns trust, with approvals and an audit log at every step.

Is my data used to train models?

No. Your production data is never used for training. Least-privilege, read-only access by default.

How is it different from Resolve, Traversal, or Cleric?

Strong tools, mostly aimed at enterprise rollouts. Vigilante is built for the team without an SRE org: fast to connect, priced for Series A–C, and honest about starting read-only.

How long to set up?

Same-day is the goal. Connect your tools with read-only access and Vigilante starts triaging that night.
