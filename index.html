Questions engineers ask

What is Vigil?

Vigil is an AI on-call engineer. It takes first response on every alert, investigates across your logs, metrics, traces, and recent deploys, and hands your team a root cause and a proposed fix. It's built for software teams with real on-call pain but no dedicated SRE org.

How is this different from PagerDuty or Datadog?

PagerDuty routes the page to a human; Datadog shows the dashboards. Vigil is the engineer who picks up the page — it reads those same signals, forms and tests hypotheses, and returns a cause and a fix. It connects to your existing PagerDuty, Opsgenie, Datadog, and Grafana instead of replacing them.

Does Vigil take actions on my production systems?

Not until you let it. Vigil is read-only by default — it investigates and proposes. As you build trust you can move it up a graduated autonomy ladder toward guarded remediation, with approvals and audit logging at every step.

Is my data used to train models?

No. Your production data is never used to train models. Vigil uses least-privilege, read-only access by default with full audit logging.

How is Vigil different from Resolve.ai, Traversal, or Cleric?

Those are strong tools aimed largely at enterprise deployments. Vigil is built for the team without an SRE army: fast to connect, priced for Series A–C, and designed around a trust ladder that starts read-only and earns autonomy — rather than an all-at-once autonomous promise or an enterprise rollout.

How long does it take to set up?

The goal is same-day. Connect your observability and paging tools with read-only access and Vigil starts triaging that night. No agents, sidecars, or schema changes required.
